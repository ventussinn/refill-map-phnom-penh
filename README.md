# Refill Map Phnom Penh (ផែនទីចាក់ទឹកស្អាត)

An interactive web app that helps students at the Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP) and people in Phnom Penh find clean drinking water refill points so they can refill reusable bottles and reduce single-use plastic waste.

## 👥 Team
- **Pair**: Sin & Co.
- **Course**: Media & Artificial Intelligence (2026–2027), Royal University of Phnom Penh (RUPP)

## 💧 What It Does
- **Interactive Map**: View clean water refill stations around RUPP Main Campus, Toul Kork, and surrounding areas.
- **Smart Filters**: Filter stations by **Free Refills**, **Open Now**, **On Campus**, and **Paid (200 riel/L)**.
- **Place Details & Real Views**: View authentic photos of each station, opening hours, verified inspection dates, and exact distance from RUPP.
- **One-Tap Directions**: Quick link directly to Google Maps navigation.
- **Bilingual Support**: Instant toggle between **ខ្មែរ (Khmer)** and **English**.
- **Suggest a Place**: Students and community members can suggest new refill locations directly to the team Telegram.
